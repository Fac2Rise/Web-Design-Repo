Flower Section

Flowers are the reproductive parts of flowering plants and are often admired for their beauty and fragrance. They come in a wide variety of shapes, sizes, and colors, each adapted to attract specific pollinators. In addition to their ecological importance, flowers have significant cultural and symbolic meanings in many societies around the world.
